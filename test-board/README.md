# The Test Board

A practice game for people who run A/B tests on email and SMS, at Fernhill Coffee Co., a made-up coffee brand with
made-up numbers. You play on copies of GrowthBook's screens and a testing board.

The path, one screen at a time:

1. **The Board**: what's running, what's next, and what can't share a week.
2. **Read one result**: GrowthBook's Results tab, one number at a time, then the call.
3. **Health** and **Overview**: is this test fit to read at all?
4. **Sizing**: the Power Calculator. How many weeks, the smallest lift a test can see, and whether it's worth running.
5. **Priority**, **Flow map**, **Roadmap**: which test goes next and why.
6. **Money** and **Slow metrics**: results in dollars, and the metrics that take months.
7. **The quarter** and **the year**: run the program.

Each step opens with a short lesson: a picture of the screen, one idea, and a guess before the answer. Then you
practise on fresh cases until most of your recent calls are right.

It's part of [Lifecycle Practice](../README.md): see the main README to run it. **Ask a question** (sidebar, or the
T key) copies your question with the step and what's on screen, ready to paste into any AI chat.

## The math

The game uses the same model as GrowthBook's calculator: a two-sided test at 95% significance, with power and the
minimum detectable effect worked out from the relative standard error of B against A.
